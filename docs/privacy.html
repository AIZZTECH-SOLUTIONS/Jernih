Jernih Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 August 2026 · Applies to the Jernih mobile app (com.aizztech.jernih)

What Jernih does

Jernih optimizes photos and videos on your device so they stay sharp when posted to WhatsApp Status. Optionally, when you sign in and enable automatic delivery, Jernih sends the optimized file to your own WhatsApp number so you can forward it to Status.

Data we collect

  • Email address — used only to sign you in (one-time passcode login).
  • WhatsApp phone number — the number you save in Settings, used only as the destination for automatic delivery.
  • Photos and videos you select — processed on your device. They are uploaded to our servers only if you enable cloud backup or automatic WhatsApp delivery.
  • Job history — file sizes, formats, and delivery status of your optimizations.

Jernih does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, contacts, or location data, and shows no ads.

How your data is used and shared

  • Media and account data are stored with Supabase (our hosting provider), protected by row-level security so only your account can access your data.
  • When automatic delivery is enabled, your optimized file and WhatsApp number are transmitted to Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) solely to send the media message to you. Meta's handling of WhatsApp messages is governed by the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • We never sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Retention

Uploaded files and job history are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Media you process without signing in never leaves your device.

Delete your account and data

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, uploaded files, job history, and WhatsApp delivery records from our servers. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

If you can no longer access the app, you can request deletion through the contact option on the app's App Store or Google Play listing, and we will remove your account and all associated data.

Children

Jernih is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
